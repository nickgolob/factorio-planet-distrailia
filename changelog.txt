Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-05-30
  Changes:
    - Distrailia now places itself through PlanetsLib (orbiting "star"), joining the shared
      orbit tree so orbit-aware mods keep it consistent. Its star-map position is unchanged
      (distance 50, beyond Secretas).
    - Registers a PlanetsLib: Tiers value (post-endgame, 6) when that mod is present, so
      tier-aware mods order Distrailia at the far edge instead of the default tier.
  Dependencies:
    - Now requires PlanetsLib; optionally uses PlanetsLib: Tiers.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-05-28
  Features:
    - M6: Added the superroboport reward - a roboport fused with a hidden substation whose power
      supply area scales with quality, from 30x30 at normal up to 50x50 at legendary (matching
      the roboport's own 50x50 logistic area at legendary). The substation is selectable, so you
      can see its supply square and open its electric-network GUI. Build, mine, deconstruct, or
      destroy it and the two stay in sync.
    - M6: Added a dedicated "Superroboport" technology (unlocks the recipe) gated behind
      reaching Distrailia.
  Info:
    - The recipe ingredients and tech cost/prerequisites are placeholders pending the M3
      resource chain (demonite / Distrailia science) and the final legendary-only gating; the
      entity, its 50x50 power area, and the build/remove lifecycle are complete.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-05-28
  Features:
    - Initial project scaffold for the Distrailia planet mod.
    - M1: Distrailia is a loadable planet (deep-copied from Nauvis as a foundation),
      with 100% solar and a Nauvis space connection.
    - M1: Distrailia is the outermost world on the star map (distance 50, beyond Secretas).
    - M1: Travel to Distrailia is gated behind a post-Aquilo discovery technology
      ("Planet discovery: Distrailia") costing 2000 cryogenic science.
    - M1: The voyage to Distrailia is dense with huge asteroids (plus some big ones, no
      promethium), ramping up on approach; its orbit stays calm when parked idle.
    - M1: Compatibility with Redrawn Space Connections - keeps Distrailia's bespoke
      huge-asteroid route instead of regenerating it (which had reduced it to medium).
